Privacy Policy

The Judge: Idle RPG
Last updated: June 4, 2026

Teriyaki Labs built The Judge: Idle RPG as an idle fantasy game with optional rewarded ads, optional in-app purchases, local profile customization, and links to community updates. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the game.

Information The Game Stores On Your Device

The game stores your progress, settings, profile name, selected avatar, currencies, upgrades, rewards, and similar gameplay state locally on your device. This local save data is used to run the game and restore your progress. Teriyaki Labs does not operate a separate account server for this information in the current release.

Rewarded Ads And Ad Data

The game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads. You choose whether to watch these ads. AdMob and related Google SDKs may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, advertising data, coarse location, product interaction data, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data. This information may be used for third-party advertising, developer advertising or marketing, analytics, app functionality, ad delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and service diagnostics.

On iOS, the game may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing the advertising identifier. You can allow or deny tracking in iOS settings. If you deny tracking, ads may still appear, but they may be less personalized.

In-App Purchases

The game offers optional diamond purchases. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device. Teriyaki Labs does not receive your full payment card details. The game receives purchase confirmation from the store so it can grant the purchased diamonds.

Discord And External Links

The game may include a link to the official Discord community. If you open that link, you leave the game and Discord's own privacy practices apply. You can review Discord's privacy policy on Discord's website.

Children's Privacy

The Judge: Idle RPG is not directed to children under 13. Teriyaki Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

Data Choices

You can manage ad tracking permission in your device settings. You can also reset or delete local game progress by clearing the app's local data or uninstalling the app. Store purchase history and payment information are managed by Apple or Google.

Service Providers

The game may use third-party services, including Google AdMob, Apple App Store, Google Play, and Discord. These services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Security

Teriyaki Labs uses reasonable measures to protect game data, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated as the game changes. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last changed.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Teriyaki Labs through the support page: The Judge Support.
